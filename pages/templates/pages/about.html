Our Mission

Bridging Clinical Care and At-Home Recovery

NOVA Physical Therapy & Solutions was created to bridge the gap between clinical physical therapy and at-home recovery. We believe that everyone deserves access to high-quality, personalized rehabilitation tools — regardless of where they are in their recovery journey.

Our platform provides exercise recommendations tailored to your specific diagnosis, pain level, and recovery phase. Every piece of content is reviewed by licensed physical therapists and medical professionals to ensure accuracy and safety.
